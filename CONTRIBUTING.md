# Contributing

Thanks for your interest in SetLoop!

- **Bug reports and ideas** are welcome in [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/PersonaN1/SetLoop/issues) or in the Telegram channel [@setnewloop](https://t.me/setnewloop).
- **Pull requests** are welcome too. For anything bigger than a small fix, please open an issue first so we can agree on the approach.
